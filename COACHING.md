# How to Use Codex as Your Coach

Copy one prompt at a time after doing the work yourself.

## Hint mode

> I'm on module __, exercise __. Here is my code and failing output. Do not give the solution. Ask one diagnostic question or give one small hint.

## Explain-back mode

> Interview me on module __. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, challenge vague statements, and score only after 5 questions.

## Code-review mode

> Review this completed exercise like a technical interviewer. Check correctness, edge cases, readability, mutation, complexity, and tests. Do not rewrite it until after I respond to the feedback.

## Assessment mode

> Start assessment __. Confirm the timer and rules, but don't reveal guidance while the timer is active. When I say time, review and score with the rubric, then conduct a 10-minute defense.

## Retake mode

> Based on my mistakes, make a new problem with the same underlying concepts but different domain/data. Do not include the answer or tests until I submit.
